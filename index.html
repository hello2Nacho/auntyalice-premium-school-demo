Our Philosophy

The one wealth that can never be taken away.

Our motto, Knowledge is True Wealth, sits at the heart of the college crest. It is a simple conviction: what a young person truly understands stays with them for life, and becomes the foundation for everything they go on to build.

Read in our crest

  1. 01

    The open book

    Learning built patiently on reading, writing and steady study, the groundwork of real understanding.

  2. 02

    The computer

    An education that looks forward, preparing students for the tools and demands of a modern world.

  3. 03

    The dove

    Knowledge carried with peace and good character, so that what students learn is used well.

About the College

A Mararaba college built around learning.

Aunty Alice College is a school community in Mararaba, gathered around one conviction: that knowledge is the truest wealth a young person can hold.

From the open book on our crest to the orange our students wear each day, the college is shaped by that idea. Our classrooms are places for patient teaching and honest effort, where learning is meant to last well beyond the school years.

Academics

Teaching that builds real understanding.

From the earliest years to secondary school, lessons at Aunty Alice College are built on reading, writing and discussion, with students encouraged to ask questions and take an active part in every class.

  1. 01

    Nursery & Day Care

    A caring first step into learning for the youngest children, building early language, number and social skills.

  2. 02

    Secondary College

    Secondary education that prepares students with the knowledge, discipline and confidence for the next stage of their lives.

  3. 03

    In every classroom

    Patient teaching, careful written work and room for every student’s voice, in bright, orderly classrooms.

School Life

Life beyond the lesson.

Between classes, along the balconies and across the compound, school life at Aunty Alice College is about the friendships, habits and shared pride that shape young people as much as any lesson.

  • Friendship & belonging

    Classrooms and corridors where students know one another and look out for each other.

  • Respect & good conduct

    The calm, considerate character that the dove on our crest stands for.

  • Pride in our colours

    The orange and pale-yellow uniform, worn with pride every school day.
