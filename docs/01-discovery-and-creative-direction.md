Orange stays an accent. The site should read as warm cream, ink and photography, with orange used for emphasis, not as the background of whole sections.

### Typography direction

- Display: a warm, slightly soft serif such as **Fraunces** (optical sizes, humane rather than corporate).
- Body and UI: a clean humanist sans such as **DM Sans**.
- This deliberately differs from MC Royal (Plus Jakarta Sans).

### Shape and treatment

- Small radii (2–6 px), hairline borders, no heavy shadows.
- Photography is full-bleed or in asymmetric editorial crops.
- Image grade: warm, natural, slightly matte. True skin tones and no teal-orange grade, so the orange uniforms stay true.

## 4. Visual identity observed in the reference photos

### Uniform specification (for every generated image)

- **Shirt:** short-sleeved, pale butter-yellow/cream, with a fine, subtle printed pattern.
- **Over-layer:** orange sleeveless V-neck knitted sweater vest, with a green-and-white horizontal band near the hem and thin multicolour trim at the armholes and neckline.
- **Trousers:** orange, matching the vest.
- **Headwear:** soft orange berets, worn by some students (likely girls).
- No blazers, no ties, and no invented crest or badge on the clothing.
- Girls' lower garment: unconfirmed (only trousers are visible). Until you confirm, frame shots so it isn't the focus.

### Architecture

- Long two-storey classroom block with open corridor balconies on both floors.
- White metal balustrade railings and square columns.
- Warm ochre/mustard-yellow walls.
- White hipped long-span roofs.
- A projecting stairwell bay with a tall blue-glass window strip.
- Open sandy compound with a few young trees.

### Classroom

- Upper walls painted blue, grey lower dado.
- White floor tiles and barred casement windows letting in strong daylight.
- Wooden desk-and-bench units on dark metal frames, and a whiteboard.

### Atmosphere

- Bright, hazy northern-central Nigerian daylight (harmattan haze is plausible).
- Orderly, modest and sincere. The premium quality has to come from light, composition and craft, not from making the school look like something it isn't.

## 5. Creative direction — "Warm Light, True Ground"

Professional Nigerian school advertising film and editorial photography, the kind a top Lagos or Abuja agency would shoot for an established school. The direction has four parts:

- **Real place, better light.** The building and classrooms keep their actual architecture, finishes and furniture. They are lit and composed beautifully, not swapped for a fantasy campus.
- **Students as the story.** Natural, unposed moments: focus, laughter and quiet confidence. Ages match the school.
- **Orange as a thread.** The uniform orange carries the brand through every frame naturally, so no extra orange props or graphics are needed.
- **Restraint.** No lens flares, no heavy bokeh, no slow-motion hair flips and no glossy American prep-school styling.

## 9. Quality gate for every generated asset

Reject and regenerate if any of these appear:

- Readable text or signage anywhere.
- Logo-like marks or badges.
- Hands with wrong finger counts, fused fingers or melted pens.
- Asymmetrical or uncanny eyes; plastic skin.
- Railings, columns or windows that bend or don't line up.
- A uniform that drifts: wrong colours, a blazer or tie, a missing green-and-white band.
- An American or European look: lawns, lockers, brick colonnades.
- Over-grading: too glossy, teal shadows, heavy bokeh.
